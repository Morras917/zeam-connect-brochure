[ S.02 ] BUILT FOR // ENTERPRISES · PAYROLL · MERCHANTS /PLATFORM/AUDIENCE
Who Zeam is for

Three audiences.
One platform underneath.

Zeam is the cross-border infrastructure layer for organisations that need money to move reliably across markets — whether you're running a multinational treasury, paying a distributed workforce, or settling marketplace flows.

01 · Enterprises & Multinationals

Treasury without a branch in every market.

Hold working balances across markets, pay suppliers in local currency, repatriate revenue, and convert between stablecoin and fiat — all from a single dashboard. No banking relationship required in every country you operate in.

Treasury Supplier payments FX Repatriation
02 · Payroll & HR platforms

Pay anyone, anywhere, in their local rails.

Embed Zeam into your payroll product and disburse to bank accounts, mobile money wallets, cash networks and vouchers — across 130+ countries — with a single API and a single reconciliation file. Built for distributed teams, gig workforces, and cross-border contractors.

Worker payouts Mobile money Bank Cash
03 · Merchants & Marketplaces

Collect locally. Settle globally.

Accept payments in local currency via card, EFT, mobile money, or QR. Settle into stablecoin for treasury, or directly into your home bank account. Pay vendors and partners through the same rails — without juggling per-country acquirers.

Collections Vendor payouts Settlement FX
